We design and build connected health devices end to end: hardware, embedded firmware, mobile apps, cloud and AI. Our wearables and non-contact sensors track sleep, breathing, heart and activity, and we take them all the way to pilot and production.

## 🛠️ What we build

- **Hardware:** low-power wearable and sensor PCBs, design for manufacture (DFM), prototyping and manufacturing in India
- **Firmware:** Nordic nRF52 / nRF54 / nRF91, Zephyr RTOS & nRF Connect SDK, ESP32, BLE, LTE-M / NB-IoT, GNSS, secure OTA/DFU
- **Signal processing & AI:** respiration, heart-rate and sleep algorithms, on-device DSP, ML models in the cloud
- **Apps & cloud:** iOS and Android companion apps, MQTT/REST backends, dashboards
